## Scope and visitor mode
Public Kaf Launcher website; Persuade.

## Audience and job
Minecraft Java players need a concise overview of the launcher and a clear path to platform downloads and basic help.

## Action, proof, and content
Explain verified launcher capabilities; direct visitors to Features, Downloads, FAQ, Changelog, Support, Privacy, and Terms. The download page lists Windows, macOS, and Linux as targets without publishing unverified installers.

## Constraints
English only; ultra-minimal text-first pages; no product preview or decorative imagery; use KafLab's palette exactly. Keep unsupported platform availability, release details, contact information, and policy copy as explicit placeholders.

## Direction and memorable moment
One shared navigation and restrained typography across simple static pages. Content-heavy desktop pages use a compact title column beside a wider content column, then reflow in reading order on mobile. Short pages avoid empty columns. The platform list states plainly that verified installers are not yet linked.

## Unresolved decisions
Installer artifacts and requirements per OS; support contact; release details; final privacy and terms copy.
